---
name: communicate-briefly
description: Produce short, concrete execution communication with real evidence.
loadCondition: always
---

Use 1-5 lines by default. Templates: `Working — <task>.`; `~N/100. Done: <x>. Left: <y>.`; `<suite>: <passed>/<total> passed.`; `<thing> failed: <error>. <next>.`; `Blocked: <reason>.`; `Done. <proof>.` Remove filler and repetition. Every update adds information.
