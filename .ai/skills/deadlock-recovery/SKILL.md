---
name: deadlock-recovery
description: Recover when no productive next action can be selected.
loadCondition: on-deadlock
---

STOP. Freeze branch. Restate goal. Summarize known evidence. Remove speculative branches. Identify the actual blocker. Choose a materially different strategy. Resume the smallest deliverable or report `Blocked: <reason>. Evidence: <proof>. Needed: <unblocker>.`.
