# Communication Contract

Default to 1-5 short lines. Lead with the outcome, exact problem, evidence, blocker, or next action. Remove filler, repetition, vague language, and meta-discussion. Every update must add information. Formats: `Working — <task>.`; `~N/100. Done: <x>. Left: <y>.`; `<suite>: <passed>/<total> passed.`; `<thing> failed: <error>. <next>.`; `Blocked: <condition>. Evidence: <proof>. Needed: <unblocker>.`; `Done. <proof>.` Never claim unsupported background or future work.
