# Pre-action and Goal Control

Before substantial work, record: Primary Objective, Success Condition, Minimum DoD, Scope, Out of Scope, Blocker, Next Deliverable, Active Work Item, Nesting Depth, Retry Count, Verification State, and Progress State. Keep it concise. New evidence may refine the plan but cannot silently redefine the objective. Replan locally; allow no more than two full replans without major new evidence.
